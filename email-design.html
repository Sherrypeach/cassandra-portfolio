How I design an email

1. Brief and goal

One clear goal per email, the audience and where it sits in the customer journey.

2. Layout in modules

Reusable blocks (hero, product grid, steps, order summary) so the team can build new emails fast.

3. Build and test

Table-based HTML, inline styles, bulletproof buttons, alt text and checks on Gmail, Outlook, Apple Mail and mobile.

4. Measure and improve

A/B tests on subject lines, CTAs and layout, then iterate on open and click results.

These are concept designs for fictional brands, made to show my process and style. Emails I designed for companies are under NDA and I'm happy to walk through them on a call.
