About me

I'm a full-stack developer and project manager. I build web and mobile apps with React, React Native, NestJS and PostgreSQL, and I've published apps to the App Store and Google Play. Since 2022 I've also worked freelance, building websites, apps and online stores for clients from small businesses to large companies, from custom e-commerce to Shopify, with a good eye for design and marketing.

I've worked with remote startups like PandoDev and Alianza Idiomas, and I'm just as comfortable meeting with stakeholders, writing specs and keeping a project on schedule as I am writing code.

Claude Code is part of my daily workflow, but I treat AI as a partner that needs direction: I plan the architecture, guide the implementation, and review and test everything it produces before it ships. I also bring several years of customer service and teaching experience, so I know how to turn unclear needs into concrete next steps.
