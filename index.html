About me

I'm a full-stack developer and project manager. I build web and mobile apps with React, React Native, NestJS and PostgreSQL, and I've published apps to the App Store and Google Play. Since 2022 I've also worked freelance, building websites and online stores for small businesses, from custom e-commerce to Shopify, with a good eye for design and marketing.

I've worked with remote startups like PandoDev and Alianza Idiomas, and I'm just as comfortable meeting with stakeholders, writing specs and keeping a project on schedule as I am writing code.

I also bring several years of customer service and teaching experience, so I know how to turn unclear needs into concrete next steps.
